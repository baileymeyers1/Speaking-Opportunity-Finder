CincyDATA is a community for data and AI professionals, rooted in Cincinnati and open to anyone, anywhere. We bring together DBAs, developers, data engineers, analysts, data scientists, and architects for practical, real-world sessions, on-premises and in the cloud, with a strong Microsoft data platform presence. Our goal is to help data professionals grow their careers through learning and networking.

We meet online on the third Wednesday of every month at 5:45 PM Eastern. Whether you’re a first-time speaker or a seasoned presenter, this is a welcoming stage to share what you know, sharpen a talk, and connect with the data community.

Submit a session and help us build something great. Come for the data. Stay for the community.

We’re especially looking for sessions on:

  • SQL Server, Azure SQL, and the Microsoft Data Platform
  • T-SQL, database design, and performance tuning
  • Database administration (DBA), internals, and high availability
  • Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and analytics
  • Data engineering, ETL/ELT, and data architecture
  • Data warehousing and lakehouse patterns
  • AI and machine learning for data professionals, including practical uses of LLMs and Copilot-style tools
  • PowerShell, DevOps, and automation for data professionals
  • Data governance, quality, and security
  • Career growth and community learning for data pros

Format: About 60-minute online sessions (lightning talks or longer sessions by arrangement). All levels welcome, from introductory (100) to deep-dive (400). Our audience is data professionals from the Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky, and Tri-State area, plus remote attendees.

Logistics: Sessions are delivered live over Teams, on the third Wednesday of the month at 5:45 PM Eastern. Speakers from anywhere are welcome. We’re a volunteer group with no speaker budget, and we’re happy to schedule a short tech check before your session.
