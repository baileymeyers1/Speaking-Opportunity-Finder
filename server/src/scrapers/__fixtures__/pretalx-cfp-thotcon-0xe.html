THOTCON 0xE - Call for Papers

This is the submission site for the THOTCON 0xE Call for Papers. There are three different length talks: 50 minutes, 25 minutes and 120 minutes. This site is also for submitting to our Call for Villages.

  • 50 minute talks are meant to be main stage type presentations. These talks will be in Track 1.

  • 25 minute talks are meant to be a very focused topic or singular idea to be shared. These talks will be in Track 2.

  • 120 minute talks are meant to be mini workshops where content should include multiple demos leave the attendees learning how to do something unique. Workshops are located in Track X.

  • Villages are an area of the conference where you host a topic-based collection of people and things. For example, the Lock Pick Village.

The THOTCON 0xE will be held in Chicago, IL on June 11th and 12th, 2027. This is on a Friday and Saturday.

Submissions open on 2026-11-05 00:00 (America/Chicago).
